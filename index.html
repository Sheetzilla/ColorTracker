🔒 colortracker / privacy

Why sign in with Google?

Signing in is how ColorTracker knows which data is yours, so your activities and sessions sync between your phone and computer.

What the app gets from Google

Your name, email address and profile picture. Nothing else: it can't see your Gmail, contacts or files.

What's saved

Your activities and the sessions you log. They're stored in Google Firebase, locked to your account. Other users can't see them.

The developer can technically access the database to fix problems, but doesn't look at or use your data.

What's never done

No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared.

On your device

The app saves a few settings, like week or month view, plus a copy of your data so it works offline.

Delete your data

Email info@sleetsheet.com and it will be removed.
