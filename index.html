Why log in?

Signing in is how ColorTracker knows which data is yours, so your activities and sessions sync between your phone and computer. You can use an email and password or your Google account.

What the app gets

With email: just your email address.

With Google: your name, email address and profile picture. Nothing else: it can't see your Gmail, contacts or files.

What's saved

Your activities and the sessions you log. They're stored in Google Firebase, locked to your account. Other users can't see them.

The developer can technically access the database to fix problems, but doesn't look at or use your data.

What's never done

No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared.

    Shown in the Edit activity window. Write minutes (90) or hours:minutes (1:30).

    Drag an activity dot onto a day to log a session. You can also drag the dots in the list to reorder them.

    Hold a square and flick it away to delete it.

    Two-finger tap: undo · Three-finger tap: redo
    (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y on a computer)

    Slide left or right on the calendar to move between months (or weeks in week view).

    Double-tap the left side of the calendar to hide or show week numbers (mobile).

    Tap the animal to shuffle its dots. Tap again to put them back in order.
